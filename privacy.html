Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Bangla Uploader is a personal tool that uploads videos I make to my own Facebook Pages and my own YouTube channel. Only the owner of those accounts uses it.

What it accesses

With my permission, it uses Google's and Meta's official sign-in to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails on my own channel and Pages. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

What it stores

It does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about anyone. The only thing kept is the sign-in token for my own accounts, stored on my own computer so uploads can run.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions or in Facebook's Business Integrations settings. Revoking it stops the tool from working; nothing else is kept.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/arifbuilds/bangla-uploader.
